**Valor** is a [[Shard\|Shard]] of [[Adonalsium\|Adonalsium]] that has not been [[/wiki/Splinter#Formation]] and whose original Vessel is the [[Dragon (cosmere)\|dragon]] god [[Medelantorius\|Medelantorius]].

## Intent
Valor's Intent as a Shard is focused on bravery and doing uncomfortable things in the name of achieving good.

## History
 
Like all Shards, Valor was created at the [[Shattering\|Shattering]] of [[Adonalsium\|Adonalsium]], and picked up by Medelantorius. Sometime after the Shattering, a shard with a dragon as Vessel, presumably Valor, found a planet and created a race of [[/wiki/Dragon (cosmere)#Lesser Dragons]]. These dragons are mortal, possess human-level intelligence, are incapable of shapeshifting and have been known to let humans ride them. It is unclear if she also created these humans, or if they were there before she arrived.
[[Tanavast\|Tanavast]] searched for her during the conflict with [[Odium\|Odium]] 3,000 years after the Shattering, but could not find her. He did not believe that she had been killed, however, as he was confident he would have felt her death.
After other efforts on different planets Valor founded the religion on [[Miral\|Miral]] that came to revere the [[Vasher\|Prophet]], in order to encourage people to do uncomfortable things in the name of good. She also laid a trap there for people like [[Hoid\|Hoid]] in one of her major temples.
Valor has had dealings with [[Endowment\|Endowment]], and at some point reached out to receive her aid in an unknown matter. Endowment had acquiesced without going to Valor's location in person, thus maintaining the Shards' agreement to isolation. This matter might have been dealing with the [[Megalith\|Megalith]] on Miral.
Some time around the [[True Desolation\|True Desolation]], on the advice of Hoid, Harmony reached out to Valor about the threat posed by [[Odium\|Odium]]; their conversation left him with the impression that she is reasonable. He later encouraged Hoid to try approaching her again, as she felt it had been too long since their last conversation. Despite this, Hoid was apparently unable to locate Valor himself, and when he asked Endowment for information on her location, Endowment refused to help, claiming that Valor had no interest in speaking to Hoid again. In Endowment's view, both she and Valor are the only Shards who still maintain a degree of isolation.
After his [[Ascension\|Ascension]] to Odium, [[Taravangian\|Taravangian]] was also unable to locate Valor, finding that she and [[Reason\|one other Shard]] had somehow completely vanished from interaction with the other Shards.
Following his work assisting [[Waxillium Ladrian\|Waxillium Ladrian]], Hoid intended to find Valor in order to assist the people of Roshar to resist [[Retribution\|Retribution]]. One of his stops was on Miral, where he was burned away with what might have been [[Godsflame\|Godsflame]] when he tried to enter Valor's temple. The temple contained murals indicating other planets that Valor had visited. Hoid continued the search by tracking down three planets ruled by dragons.
