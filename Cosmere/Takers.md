The **Takers** is likely the name of [[Tukks\|Tukks's]] squad of [[Alethi\|Alethi]] soldiers in [[Amaram\|Amaram's]] army on [[Roshar\|Roshar]]. The origin of the name "Takers" is unclear.
It was [[Kaladin\|Kaladin's]] first squad after he joined the army in late 1168. Sergeant Tukks notably emphasized the need to care while fighting and to avoid staying completely emotionless. The squad was composed of [[Darkeyes\|darkeyes]], and fought using spears. Outside of battle, they also trained new soldiers, both with spear and with quarterstaff. Their methods of discipline included cleaning duty and corporal punishment.
During Kaladin's tenure with the Takers, he refined his talent with the spear into proper skill. At some point, either most or all of them died, and Kaladin was transferred elsewhere, perhaps straight to leading [[Kaladin's squad\|his own squad]]. Kaladin regretted being unable to save his squadmates, and often thinks about Tukks's philosophy regarding fighting with purpose.
