**William Montane** is the late partner of [[Silence Montane\|Silence (Forescout) Montane]] and the father of [[William Ann Montane\|William Ann Montane]].
Little is known about William Montane. [[Silence's grandmother\|Silence's grandmother]] is familiar with him, and it is possible that William worked at [[Silence's waystop\|the Forescout waystop]] when she was running it. Silence's grandmother considers him to be a fool and an idiot, and she believes that Silence is better off after his death. It is implied that William and Silence may have shared some opinions about the [[God Beyond\|God Beyond]] that her grandmother considered blasphemous.
Silence becomes pregnant by William shortly before his death, although it is uncertain how he died. Silence assumes that her grandmother will condemn the pregnancy, although she seems to take it in stride. When Silence gives birth to her daughter, she names the girl William Ann after her father. William Ann is tall, slender, and pretty; she is said to take after her father in appearance. There is no record of William and Silence being legally married. Nevertheless, Silence takes his surname, possibly in an attempt to protect William Ann from the social stigma of being born out of wedlock.
After William's death, his shade is said to guard Silence's waystop against enemies, although it is possible that this legend was started by Silence herself.
