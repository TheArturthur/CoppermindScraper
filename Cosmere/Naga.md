**Naga** is a [[Taynix\|taynix]] found on [[Detritus\|Detritus]]. Naga is partners with Arturo. Naga goes with [[Arturo\|Arturo]] to [[Blightfall\|Blightfall]]. After [[Arturo\|Arturo]] escapes from the superiority station, Naga is given permission from [[Bob\|Bob]] to hyperjump him to the surface. He hyperjumps him near were  landed. Naga helps [[Bob\|Bob]] save several taynix from [[Ivy\|Ivy]]. Naga is very sympathetic, and doesn’t like it when her friends are sad.
