**Garmet** is a city in the very southeastern portion of the [[Elendel Basin\|Elendel Basin]]. It is adjacent to the [[Seran Range\|Seran Range]] and southeast of [[Elmsdel\|Elmsdel]]. Garmet is a perfect example of [[Elendel\|Elendel]]’s economic policies. Because the railways and canals all go towards Elendel, stone quarried in Garmet has to travel five hundred miles before it reaches [[New Seran\|New Seran]], while the two are only one hundred miles apart overland.
