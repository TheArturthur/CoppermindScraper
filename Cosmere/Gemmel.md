## Appearance and Personality
 
Gemmel is a scrawny older man with a ragged gray beard and long, unkempt hair. Like most Mistborn, he wears a [[Mistcloak\|mistcloak]] on nightly escapades. Kelsier notices that Gemmel somehow looks 'regal' and more grand than his usual appearance in the right light with his mistcloak on.
Gemmel is touched by [[Ruin\|Ruin]], which presumably affects his personality. He does not appear to be completely sane. He often mutters to himself or converses with a voice in his head, and sometimes seems more beast than man. He does not smile often, and is very brusque; he often stops talking in the middle of an argument. He doesn't understand humor, and does not like it when his pupil tries to take control. He is a ruthless teacher; he pushed Kelsier off of a building to force him to learn how to use iron and steel, and often Pushes coins at him with deadly speed.
He is likely a [[Skaa\|skaa]] halfblood. He seems to have no qualms about killing [[Noble\|nobles]] or their guards.

## Attributes and Abilities
Gemmel is a skilled Mistborn. He prefers to use [[Steel\|Steelpushes]], [[Pewter\|pewter]], and other physical Allomantic powers. He generally dislikes using mental metals such as [[Zinc\|zinc]] and [[Brass\|brass]], but will do so as needed. He is extremely confident in his abilities and sees himself as effectively invincible. He appears to be relatively knowledgeable regarding Allomancy, but his unhinged demeanor leaves Kelsier unsure about how much he can trust his teachings.

## History
Gemmel's history prior to meeting Kelsier is not known. He trained three other people in Allomancy before meeting Kelsier. He was "directed" to meet Kelsier around 1019 [[Final Empire\|FE]] as part of a master plan by [[Ruin\|Ruin]] to manipulate events on Scadrial. Kelsier knew he needed training, and Gemmel was the only Mistborn he knew that was not a [[Noble\|nobleman]].
His training of Kelsier began with Steelpushing and Ironpulling. They traveled to the west, as far as Kelsier had been at that point in his life.

### Keep Shezler
Three months after [[Kelsier\|Kelsier]] escaped from the [[Pits of Hathsin\|Pits of Hathsin]], he and Gemmel visited [[Mantiz\|Mantiz]] in the [[Western Dominance\|Western Dominance]]. Gemmel had a plan to infiltrate [[Keep Shezler\|Keep Shezler]], only telling Kelsier that they would find "something important" there. On their way to the keep, they spoke about Kelsier’s past; all the while, Gemmel kept training his student, which included attacking him.
Upon reaching the keep, Gemmel went to the roof to get a better view, killing some guards along the way. When he had taken a good look around and Kelsier has caught up to him, Gemmel threw his pupil off the roof, and Kelsier barely survived. Once at ground level, they walked across the keep's grounds to a building that looked like a groundskeeping shed. Gemmel killed more guards, not caring that he might reveal his presence. In the shed, they found a stairway down, and Gemmel waved Kelsier to go first.
In the basement, Kelsier started sneaking through the room, but Gemmel soon entered without any attempt at stealth. In the next room, they found half a dozen bound [[Skaa\|skaa]] who said that they were being subjected to experimentation with metals. Kelsier began tending to them, but was interrupted when [[Antillius Shezler\|Antillius Shezler]], the keep’s nobleman who was also Mistborn, shouted and shot a coin at him. After a brief duel, Kelsier managed to kill Shezler without any help from Gemmel, who seemed to view the incident as a training opportunity. When Kelsier returned to the [[Skaa\|skaa]] captives, Gemmel was leafing through one of Shezler’s notebooks containing theories and superstitions about an [[Malatium\|Eleventh Metal]]. Kelsier took the book before leaving, and Gemmel pocketed a fork.
Kelsier concluded that Gemmel had contrived the mission to the keep in order to give him experience fighting another Mistborn. Although this was partly true, the real reason for the mission was Ruin planting the seed with Kelsier that the Eleventh Metal could be used to defeat the [[Lord Ruler\|Lord Ruler]].
Gemmel's fate after Keep Shezler is unclear. While traveling in the [[Cognitive Realm\|Cognitive Realm]] a few years later, Kelsier encountered the soul of a madman that reminded him of Gemmel, helping him realize that his mentor had been under Ruin's influence.

## Relationships
### Kelsier
>“*I wonder what I’ve unleashed upon the world by teaching you Allomancy. Of course, my trainer said the same thing about me.*”
\-Kelsier to Vin[8]

Gemmel is Kelsier's mentor in the Allomantic arts. His training primarily focuses on the Pushing and Pulling metals, [[Steel\|steel]] and [[Iron\|iron]] respectively; he largely eschews emotional Allomancy. He helps Kelsier to think like an Allomancer, for example, he presses him to have all eight basic metals burning constantly. He frequently places Kelsier in life or death scenarios, like pushing him off a city wall. He also influenced Kelsier to use his powers to physically overwhelm his enemies with violence, rather than using elaborate plans or Soothing.
Gemmel's personality is incompatible with Kelsier's. He does not understand humor, does not like to talk much, and he thinks that Kelsier's forced smiles look creepy. He openly wondered what he was creating by teaching Kelsier Allomancy, and declined to give him a [[Mistcloak\|mistcloak]]. Although he often grumbles at Kelsier, Gemmel likes him more than his previous three apprentices, sometimes even showing some signs of respect for him. Kelsier hates Gemmel and doesn't fully trust him, but he knows that there is no one else available to train him.

### Ruin
Gemmel is "touched by" [[Ruin\|Ruin]]; this is likely due to an unseen [[Hemalurgic\|Hemalurgic]] spike or possibly because he is mentally unstable. He hears and tries to interact with a voice that no one else hears, and the [[Mist\|mists]] aren't drawn to him like they are to most Allomancers - a known side effect of Hemalurgy.
Through Ruin, Gemmel knew things he probably couldn't have figured out by himself, like certain aspects about Kelsier's past or where to find information about the Eleventh Metal. Ruin's influence also affects Gemmel's personality. At first sight, he just seems a madman, but a lot of what he says to Kelsier is very similar to things that Ruin might say. This applies especially to his answer on Kelsier's sarcastic question about the meaning of life.

>“*Don't know it. I think it's so we can die.*”
\-Gemmel's take on the meaning of life[1]

## Trivia
In a postscript to *The Eleventh Metal* in *[[Arcanum Unbounded\|Arcanum Unbounded]]*, Brandon states that he wanted to include a story about Gemmel because it helps explain how Ruin manipulated Kelsier.
Gemmel met [[Zane Venture\|Zane Venture]] once, but that meeting wasn't necessarily relevant.
Fighting Kelsier to the death as a final piece of training seems like something Gemmel would do.
