**Vapor** is a [[Figment\|figment]] who lives on Starsight and who works for [[Cuna\|Cuna]]. Vapor may or may not be her actual name, but she requests that her flight call her that.

## History
Vapor was one of the many pilots who participated in a practice battle organized by [[Winzik\|Winzik]] to weed out less suitable pilots. She inhabited a small Krell drone during the battle. When Morrimur’s fighter was incapacitated and Spensa sent out a distress call, Vapor agreed to help as long as Spensa didn’t shoot her. At Spensa’s request, Vapor defended Morrimur from enemy ships as Spensa worked to remove a chunk of debris from Morrimur’s booster.
After the test battle, Vapor was assigned to [[Spensa\|Spensa]], [[Brade\|Brade]], and [[Hesho\|Hesho]]’s flight, [[Flight Fifteen\|Flight Fifteen]], and introduced herself to them. Vapor is assigned a sniper ship, and Spensa suggested Vapor to become their flightleader, after noticing how comfortable Vapor is with leadership, so much so that it interferes with her shots. Vapor, in turn, noticed Spensa's military experience after she privately contacts Vapor regarding drills, and assigned her to be assistant flightleader. The two of them worked together to train the rest of their flight on basic formations. At some point during their training, both Vapor and Spensa explored the inactive delver maze together in order to better understand what they are up against. They used their experience in order to enhance their own flight's training.
Later on, Vapor ended up helping Spensa escape *[[Weights and Measures\|Weights and Measures]]* lockdown due to her chaotic attempt to extract the secret to the Superiority's hyperdrive. It is revealed that Vapor is working for [[Cuna\|Cuna]], and she knew who Spensa really is, but she hasn't told anyone the truth, leading Spensa to reveal herself and started working with Cuna. Before Spensa is able to work out an escape off Starsight, Vapor informed her that the Superiority is mobilizing their flight, figuring that a delver might have been spotted. Vapor and Spensa then joins other pilots in *Weights and Measures*, realizing that they are being mobilized to [[Detritus\|Detritus]] instead.
During the Battle of Detritus, Spensa convinced Vapor to help her stop Brade from summoning the delver, and they, alongside Hesho, worked together to try and stop her. Vapor was able to take control of Brade's starfighter for a moment after losing hers, allowing Spensa to take a shot at Brade. However, their attempts fail as the delver gets summoned, and Vapor managed to survive through the delver's attack before it gets sent to Starsight. Spensa then brought Vapor and Hesho's crew to Starsight, and they attempt to find the heart of the delver, but Vapor ended up losing contact with Spensa.
After the battle is over, Vapor gave her battle report to Cuna, attempting to explain what they know regarding their experience in the delver maze.
Vapor is one of the people Spensa unknowingly reached out to cytonically when she started having breakdown after losing [[Rebecca Nightshade\|Gran-Gran]] in the Battle of Evensong. Vapor sensed Spensa's mind, and although she is worried, she reached out and comforted her.
