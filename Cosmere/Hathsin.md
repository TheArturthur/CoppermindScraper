The **Pits of Hathsin** are a system of caves near [[Luthadel\|Luthadel]] on [[Scadrial\|Scadrial]] at the time of the [[Final Empire\|Final Empire]] in which [[Atium\|atium]] geodes grow. 

## Geography
The Pits are known to the people of the [[Final Empire\|Final Empire]] only as a deadly [[Skaa\|skaa]] labor camp the [[Lord Ruler\|Lord Ruler]] set up, but it actually also produces [[Atium\|atium]] crystals. It serves the dual purpose of punishing [[Skaa\|skaa]] criminals and concealing the location of the atium mines, as the prisoners cannot leave. During the events of the Final Empire, the Pits of Hathsin are the only known place where atium can be mined. Imprisoned skaa are required to climb down a suffocatingly narrow gorge and reach into crystal-lined niches to find the geodes that contain the atium. Each skaa has to find an atium geode every week; if they fail, they are savagely beaten to death.
The Pits are also the location of a [[Ruin's Perpendicularity\|Perpendicularity]] that is the liquid form of [[Ruin\|Ruin]], as the [[Well of Ascension\|Well of Ascension]] is to [[Preservation\|Preservation]]. The Pits of Hathsin were used for interplanetary trade by [[Worldhoppers\|worldhoppers]], which [[Rashek\|Rashek]] was aware of.

## History
The Pits of Hathsin were created by [[Preservation\|Preservation]] to hide Ruin's Perpendicularity.
[[Kelsier\|Kelsier]] was the first known person to survive imprisonment in the Pits. This feat gained him the title, "The Survivor of Hathsin". He was forced into labor there along with his wife [[Mare\|Mare]] as punishment for attempting to infiltrate and rob the Lord Ruler's palace, [[Kredik Shaw\|Kredik Shaw]]. After Mare died in the Pits, Kelsier [[Snap\|Snapped]] and escaped using his newfound abilities as a [[/wiki/Allomancer#Mistborn]].
Several days before the fall of the [[Final Empire\|Final Empire]], Kelsier returned to the Pits. There, he set free the enslaved skaa prisoners and destroyed most of Hathsin's atium-producing crystals using Allomancy. His iron was enough to detect trace amounts of atium in the crystals, and Pulling on them shattered the crystals with kinetic force. Afterward, he remarked that his actions would likely end atium production in the Final Empire for the next three hundred years, as the crystals would need time to regrow before any more atium could be mined.
After the Collapse of the Final Empire and the end of the atium mining, the Pits in the Central Dominance became the new home of the Terris people, as the remaining infrastructure from the old mining camp had buildings, shelters, and most importantly, fresh water along with farm crops of resilient hardy plants adapted to ash that need little water. Once settled, the Terris people improved the valley, building more structures, brushing ash from the plant foliage to provide grazing for the adapted short-legged sheep that roamed the hills, and returning to their heritage of herding, before the Lord Ruler's Ascension. The Terris people's lives had become easier than most on Scadrial, living in pastoral villages that have replaced the once brutal prison camps.
After the events of the [[Catacendre\|Catacendre]], atium was no longer formed within the crystals of the Pits; the atium may or may not regrow in the future. The atium did not grow at Wax's time. However, it is possible that Harmony is doing something to reduce Ruin's power to balance out the extra energy that Preservation Invested in humankind. By this time, the Pits are also sometimes known as the **Survivor's Cradle** for their role in Kelsier's Snapping.
