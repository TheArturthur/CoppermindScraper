**Drypost** is a city in the [[Elendel Basin\|Elendel Basin]]. It is far to the north and east of [[Elendel\|Elendel]], and lies on the railroad leading into the Northern [[Roughs\|Roughs]]. In 342 [[Catacendre\|PC]], [[Dav Brayshaw\|Dav Brayshaw]], a farmer from Drypost, uncovered a rich gold vein on his land.
