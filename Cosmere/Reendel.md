Underlestrell **Reendel** is a [[Sand mastery\|sand master]] on [[Taldain\|Taldain]]. He is the son of the craftsman [[Keshdel\|Keshdel]].
Reendel was present during the advancement ceremony prior to the [[Kerztian\|Kerztian]] surprise attack. [[Elorin\|Elorin]] presented him with a purple sash to signify his advancement to the rank of [[Diem\|underlestrell]]. This means that Reendel can likely control between six and ten ribbons of sand. It is unclear if he survived the attack by the Kerztians.
