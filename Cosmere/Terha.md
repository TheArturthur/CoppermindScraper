The **terha** (pl. **terhan**) is a domesticated [[Sandling\|sandling]] on the [[Dayside\|Dayside]] of [[Taldain\|Taldain]].

## Appearance
The terha is a large quadruped. The body size and shape is similar to a rhinoceros, with a thickly muscled body and thick legs. It is covered in brown carapace, with a large, oval-shaped head that has two large horns or tusks in the front and rounded armor in the back that helps protect the neck. Plates of carapace hang from its long tail, with a particularly large triangle-shaped plate at the end. It has clawed feet that appear to have opposable thumbs.

## Usage
Terhan are domesticated; like [[Tonk\|tonks]], they are ridden using a saddle, bridle, and saddlebags. Terhan are both larger and faster than tonks; they are also tougher and less skittish. Terhan are therefore favored as mounts by warriors and hunting parties that venture into the [[Deep sand\|deep sand]]. While tonks are used extensively for general transportation, terhan are relatively rare; [[Kenton\|Kenton]] has never ridden a terha before joining Lord [[Raagent\|Raagent]] for a sandling hunt. Terhan that are raised to hunt are treated with a special substance called [[DoKall\|DoKall]] from infancy; this treatment prevents their carapace from dissolving when it gets wet.

## Taxonomy
Terha have superficial similarities to both tonks and other species of sandlings, such as horns and water-soluble carapace. It is unknown if there is a relation, natural or bred, between the different species.
