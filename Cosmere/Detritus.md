**Detritus** is a once-abandoned planet surrounded in a multi-layer artificial defense grid. It is home to the descendants of the crew of the [[Defiant (starship)\|Defiant]], one of the last surviving human spaceships. Chased through space by the alliance of aliens opposed to human conquest, [[Spensa's great-grandmother\|Spensa's great-grandmother]] guided the Defiant onto Detritus as her dying act. Since landing, the planet has become a prison for humans. The population of the *Defiant* and its fleet have been trapped on the planet for seventy five years. Most of the population is gathered in the caverns near the initial landing site of the fleet, though there are enclaves all over the planet.
Detritus's debris field has old guns, called autocannons, that are still functional and will shoot down any large ship that gets too close, making it so that only small, maneuverable ships can get through. The shielding was known to interfere with communication, though it is unknown if this refers to [[Cytonics\|cytonic]] messages or normal radio signals, as well.
The planet was inhabited by other humans before, though it wasn't called Detritus during that time. Later on, the planet was known about but avoided. This was because humans were known to be experimenting with summoning [[Delver\|Delvers]], one experiment which summoned a Delver and razed the planet of all biological life.
Detritus is smaller than Earth. It is a blue-grey color. The planet does not have any oceans.
The rats inhabiting the caverns are not native, and were there before the Defiants crashed on it.
According to M-Bot, the shipyards that can be found in Detritus's atmosphere were previously used to manufacture starships.

## Defense Grid Technology
Detritus' defense grid is equipped with multiple highly advanced technologies, conventional and cytonic, that allow the planet to act as a wandering space fortress:

### Force-field
The defense grid is equipped with an advanced shield array that can cover the entire planet. It is looks like a thin, blue film (up close it is a "crackling, glowing net") that stretches over and between the orbital platforms. The shield is capable of completely deflecting [[/wiki/Starfighter#Destructors]]. Flying through the shield can interfere with starfighter controls and instruments at best and fry the pilots inside at worst. Activating this shielding also creates a cytonic inhibitor field, presumably powered by an [[Taynix\|inhibitor slug]], that prevents the use of cytonic abilities inside it (including hyperjumping into and out of it) without the proper "cytonic key."

### Hyperjump Capabilities
Detritus' defense grid is also has a platform containing a wall of hyperdrive boxes for [[Taynix\|taynix]]. The hyperdrive system is wired to allow the entire planet, defense grid and debris field included, to hyperjump. It additionally possesses a highly advanced [[GravCap\|GravCap]] system that allows for Detritus to exist in close proximity to other planetary bodies by negating its gravitational effect, where normally this would rip apart both planets. However, this system is imperfect, either due to the defense grid's disrepair or inherent limitations, and currently can still cause tsunamis just by existing near a planet.
Additionally, individual platforms are able to independently hyperjump and form a planetary shield between the platforms, allowing for a versatile tactical maneuvers such as trapping enemy fighters beneath the shield for ally fighters to pick them off, with those above the shield to be destroyed by the autocannons.


### Weapons
The space-facing side of the platforms are equipped with automatic turrets, known as autocannons, that are still functional and will shoot down any large ship that gets too close, making it so that only small, maneuverable ships can get through.
The defense grid platforms are also equipped with [[/wiki/Cytonics#Mindblades]], powered by [[/wiki/Taynix#Boomslugs]], that allows the platforms to fire giant [[/wiki/Cytonics#Mindblades]] at opponents, which can bypass force-fields.
