Lord **Janarle** is a [[Noble\|noble]] from the [[Final Empire\|Final Empire]] on [[Scadrial\|Scadrial]]. He serves as a general in [[House Venture\|House Venture's]] army. After [[Straff Venture\|Straff Venture's]] defeat, Janarle swears fealty to [[Elend Venture\|Elend Venture]] and is promoted to King of the [[Northern Dominance\|Northern Dominance]] in the [[New Empire\|New Empire]]. Due to the [[Skaa\|skaa]] revolt in his capital of [[Urteau\|Urteau]], he rules in exile; he eventually attempts to flee the dominance as it is thrown into chaos. He is subsequently captured and turned into a [[Koloss\|koloss]].

## Appearance and Personality
He is handsome and relatively young. He seems to be very loyal and deferential to Straff, but this is likely due to his fear of [[Zane\|Zane]]. Straff believes that Janarle is ambitious and would potentially turn on him if he had the opportunity. Like many nobles, he seems to be self-centered and somewhat entitled; he also holds grudges against his rivals.

## Attributes and Abilities
Janarle displays some skill in military strategy and leadership. He often rides a horse during battles.
After he becomes a territorial king, he is not very effective at dealing with the problems facing his Dominance; he cannot deal with the stress of the job.
As a koloss, he presumably has the typical abilities of the species, including enhanced size and strength.

## History
### Venture Army
Janarle was a minor lord and career soldier. His family presumably hailed from the Northern Dominance, where House Venture was the most influential. He distinguished himself in the Venture army, as he rose to the rank of general and became Straff Venture's second-in-command.
With Straff, he took part in the [[Siege of Luthadel\|Siege of Luthadel]]. While Straff was incapacitated due to poisoning, Janarle led the army and planned a strike to be executed with Straff's permission. He also sent scouts to follow [[Vin\|Vin]], but was forced to call them back when they got too far away He continued to report the progress of the koloss attack against Luthadel. Straff was surprised that Janarle had not killed him or attempted a coup, but he realized that Zane's disappearance had remained a secret. Janarle continued to lead the army alongside Straff, and discussed the army's return to Luthadel after the koloss onslaught. When Straff decided to let the koloss raze the city, Janarle objected, but ultimately did not defy Straff. He was present at the conclusion of the [[Battle of Luthadel\|Battle of Luthadel]].

### New Empire
At the end of the Battle of Luthadel, Vin killed all of the leaders of the Venture army except Janarle. He had suffered a broken leg in the fighting. He initially pledged his army to Vin, but she told him to swear fealty to Elend instead. After the battle, he was elevated to the position of King of the [[Northern Dominance\|Northern Dominance]] and was kept loyal due to his fear of Vin. He argued with [[Ashweather Cett\|Ashweather Cett]] about the priorities of the [[New Empire\|New Empire]]. Janarle believed they should concentrate on retaking [[Urteau\|Urteau]]. The capital of his dominance had been taken over by [[Skaa\|skaa]] in a revolt led by [[Quellion\|Quellion]] during the fighting in Luthadel. Urteau was relatively close and the empire needed its resources. The empire was ultimately unable to retake Urteau; Elend could not spare enough troops to successfully lay siege to the city. Urteau remained the capital of the Northern Dominance, but Janarle ruled in exile, using a smaller army to retain control of the rest of the dominance.
Janarle's rule as king was a difficult one. He had been coerced into serving the empire and was unable to recapture the largest and most profitable city in his kingdom. Although Janarle, Cett, and [[Ferson Penrod\|Ferson Penrod]] knew that their squabbling was not productive, they could not help themselves; their agendas were self-serving and spiteful, and did not serve the empire.
Janarle also survived several assassination attempts and had to deal with koloss raiding his lands and killing his subjects. He was a military rather than political leader, and was not prepared for the stress of being king.
After [[Ruin\|Ruin]] began causing the [[Ashmount\|ashmounts]] to erupt more often, Janarle gave up and abdicated his throne. He packed supplies and attempted to flee for the hills near [[Terris\|Terris]] with his family and retinue. The Northern Dominance was left in chaos.

### Koloss
On his way through the Northern Dominance, Janarle's party was attacked by [[Koloss\|koloss]]. The survivors, including Janarle, were transformed into koloss themselves. As a koloss, Janarle lived in the [[Central Dominance\|Central Dominance]] and presumably took part in the [[Battle of Hathsin\|Battle of Hathsin]]. As he did not make it to the front lines, he likely died when the sun rose and began burning the land.

## Trivia
Janarle's instinct to flee can be contrasted with Elend, who was also experiencing a massive amount of stress but decided to stay and fight.
Brandon cites Janarle as an example of a side character that serves their purpose (in this case, submitting to Vin and Elend) and does not need to be explored further. Janarle is mentioned but does not appear in *[[The Hero of Ages\|The Hero of Ages]]*; his abdication and transformation into a koloss happen off-screen, and these events are only known through the annotations.
