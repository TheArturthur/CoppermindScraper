**Tremredare** is a large city in the [[Western Dominance\|Western Dominance]] of the [[Final Empire\|Final Empire]]. It is one of [[Scadrial\|Scadrial's]] major cultural centers before the [[Collapse\|Collapse]].
It is located in the southwest portion of the Dominance, west of [[Morag\|Morag]] and near the base of a mountain range. A river or canal connects Tremredare to [[Lake Tyrian\|Lake Tyrian]] via [[Chardees\|Chardees]] and [[Fadrex City\|Fadrex City]]. Another arm of the same waterway connects to the [[River Channerel\|River Channerel]]. Tremredare is heavily staffed with obligators. Its markets generate a nice revenue for the dominance.
Tremredare served as the capital of the Western Dominance until [[Ashweather Cett\|Ashweather Cett]] seized power in the area and moved the capital to Fadrex City since it was smaller and more defensible.
Tremredare is the primary city in the Final Empire where nobles can purchase goods made by their own social class. The sprawling city boast showrooms for fine fashions, bespoke pottery, decorative dueling canes, and exquisite paintings. It is common for nobles from other dominances to send stewards to Tremredare to shop for them. After whispers spread of noble showrooms selling skaa-made goods as their own, obligators raided and shut down multiple showrooms. Even more concerning, they found artifacts of communication between rebels in many items, sewn into garments and hidden in paintings. After that, obligator art auditors regularly perused the Tremredare markets from dawn until dusk in search of criminal activity. While the skaa also produced fine goods, those were sold at much lower prices and weren’t celebrated for their craft. To avoid competition, nobles prohibited skaa from selling handicrafts that were also made by nobles. Skaa artisans often produced knickknacks based on their superstitions, such as small [[Sunwraith\|sunwraith]] guardian effigies.
Tremredare is the eponym of the [[Post-Catacendre\|Post-Catacendre]] city [[New Tremredare\|New Tremredare]].
